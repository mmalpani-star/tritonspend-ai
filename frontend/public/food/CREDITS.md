# Food photo credits

All food photos in this folder are openly licensed images from Wikimedia Commons (CC0, CC BY or
CC BY-SA; many are Unsplash photos mirrored on Commons under CC0), resized for the web. The
machine-readable manifest — author, license, license URL and source page for every file — is
`frontend/lib/food-photos.ts`, and the app shows the credits under "Photo credits" in the footer.

None of these photos were taken at UC San Diego. They are always presented as illustrative and are
never shown as a dish a particular venue serves.
